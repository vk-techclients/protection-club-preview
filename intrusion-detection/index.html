FAQ

Will my pets set off the alarm?

Our motion sensors are tuned to ignore everyday activity, pets included, while recognizing real threats — detection without constant false alarms.

Can I control the system from my phone?

Yes — arm and disarm remotely, receive instant alerts, and check status in real time from the app; at the property, the touchscreen keypad does the same.

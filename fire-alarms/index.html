Code-Compliant Fire Alarm Systems

Ensure your business meets all state and local fire safety codes with a professionally installed fire alarm system. We design systems that comply with NFPA regulations and AHJ guidelines — around your building, your occupancy, and your local authority's requirements, so inspection day holds no surprises.

Inspections & Maintenance That Keep You Compliant

A fire alarm only does its job if it works when you need it. Scheduled maintenance, testing, and repairs catch small issues before they become failures — and when a device fails a test or your AHJ flags a deficiency, we fix it and document the work.

Upgrades & Migration, Without Disruption

Aging, unsupported, or failing inspections? We redesign and migrate existing systems to modern requirements while your business keeps running. Panels and devices can be replaced in phases — your property is never left unwatched, and spending follows your long-term safety goals.

Watched Around the Clock

An alarm that only makes noise is easy to ignore. Monitoring means a signal — fire or burglar — is received and acted on, 24 hours a day, 7 days a week, whether you are on the property, across town, or asleep.

Cellular Signal, Battery Backup

Your system reports over a cellular connection, so a cut phone line or a down internet connection doesn't silence it. And when the power goes out, battery backup keeps your system reporting — storms and outages are exactly when monitoring matters most.

Alerts That Reach You

When your system speaks up, you hear about it: alarm events and system alerts go straight to your phone, so you know what happened and when — not hours later.

FAQ

Questions, answered.

What does 24/7 monitoring cover?

Fire alarms and burglar alarms, around the clock. When a signal comes in — smoke, intrusion, a system alert — it is received and acted on, and you are alerted.

Does monitoring work during a power outage?

Yes. Your system reports over a cellular signal and runs on battery backup when the power is out, so monitoring continues through outages.

Can you monitor the system I already have?

In many cases, yes. We review your existing system during a free consultation and tell you plainly whether it can be monitored as-is or what it would take.

What does monitoring cost?

It starts with a free consultation and a written estimate for your approval — no work begins until you approve it.

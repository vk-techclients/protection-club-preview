FAQ

Questions, answered.

Can I watch my cameras from my phone?

Yes — live feeds from phone, tablet, or computer, and the app lets you review, save, and share clips from wherever you are.

How do you decide where cameras go?

We map your layout first and place each camera at entrances, high-traffic areas, and risk-assessed locations — never by a one-size template.

Do you repair existing camera systems?

Yes — installation, repair, and maintenance. Many issues are fixed remotely; if not, a technician visits your property.

What does it cost — and are you licensed?
